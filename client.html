Property Protection

Protect what you've acquired.

Beloveeth doesn't stop at helping you buy — protection is what happens after.

Property Protection is a premium service involving the appropriate legal and insurance structure for an eligible property. Where available, it can be purchased as a one-time premium per property rather than a recurring platform subscription. Beloveeth's legal/verification workflow determines eligibility and status — Proppy can explain what a status means, but does not decide it.
